📜 Sovereign Arena: How to Play

Welcome to the Sovereign Arena! Manage your tactical cards and conquer campaign tiers:

1. Stance & Weapon Switching: Cards like Kick Strike (Sword ⚔️), Deploy Shield (Shield 🛡️), or Phoenix Eagle (Eagle 🦅) transform your Sovereign Guardian's stance in real-time.

2. Touch & Clash Combat: Clicking a card sends your block marching forward to physically touch and clash with the enemy.

3. Shop & Local Storage: All progress, stats, and gold coins are automatically saved to local storage. If you need more coins, visit the shop!
